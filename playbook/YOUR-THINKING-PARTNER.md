# Your Thinking Partner

You have an idea. Maybe it's half-formed. Maybe you've been
circling it for weeks. Maybe you just need to think it through
without losing your place.

That's what this is for.

Your thinking partner helps you develop ideas — any kind of idea —
by asking good questions, remembering what you've decided, and
picking up exactly where you left off every time you come back.

It doesn't take over. It doesn't tell you what to do.
It just makes sure your thinking gets clearer every session.

---

To start:

Tell your thinking partner what you're working on
and what you need help figuring out.

That's it.
