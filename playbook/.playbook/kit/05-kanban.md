## Rules
- Tickets come from Acceptance Criteria in 03-execution.md
- One outcome per ticket — no bundling
- Priority within each column is top to bottom
- Blocked tickets stay in their column — what is blocking them is stated explicitly
- When a ticket moves to Done, log it in 04-progress.md
