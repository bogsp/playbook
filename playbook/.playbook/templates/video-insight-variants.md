# Video Insight — Extra Sections by Type
# File: .kit/_refs/video-insights/video-insight-variants.md

Add one of these sections after ## Key Points based on the video type.
Use the matching Gemini prompt variant from guides/video-insights.md.

---

## Tutorial or How-to → add ## Steps

### Steps
The process being taught, in order.
1. [Step]
2. [Step]
3. [Step]

---

## Case Study → add ## Outcome

### Outcome
What happened, what it proved, and what the result means for someone applying it.

---

## Framework or System → add ## Framework

### Framework
The model or system being presented.
How it works, what problem it solves, and how it is applied in practice.

---

## Opinion or Commentary → add ## Counterarguments

### Counterarguments
What pushes back on the central argument.
Known objections, limitations, or blind spots — whether the creator acknowledges them or not.

---

## Research or Data → add ## Evidence

### Evidence
The data points, studies, or sources cited.
What the data actually shows versus what is being claimed from it.
